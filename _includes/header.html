{%- comment -%} Site header: the wordmark (which is also the way home), the four section links and a button for /openings. aria-current marks the section the page belongs to: "page" on the section's own page, "true" on the pages under it (a member's profile under Team). It wraps onto a second line on a phone rather than collapsing behind a menu button: there are only five targets.
